Completed: 2026-09-04

Production application commit: `05fa7cf`

Branch: `seo/full-remediation-2026`

Baseline commit: `f173285b073020c4875090c1e066ad65c056471e`

## 1. Executive summary

The audit confirmed a severe index-quality and trust problem rather than a missing-tags problem. Production exposed 2,500 synthetic reviews as verified purchases, submitted all 4,773 products and all 20 unreviewed health articles to search engines, depended on 4,723 externally hosted product images, mixed unrelated carbohydrate products into Gainers, rendered Markdown and skincare attributes on sports products, served both apex and `www`, and returned soft-404 catalogue responses.

The remediation preserved the commerce catalogue and all database rows while sharply reducing what search engines are invited to index. Synthetic reviews and unreviewed articles were quarantined, an explainable product-quality ledger was added, 4,759 low-confidence products were changed to `noindex,follow`, the sitemap fell from 5,252 to 41 verified 200 URLs, unsupported absolute claims were made index blockers, the brand directory became server-rendered, pagination became crawlable, product rendering and schema were corrected, the canonical host was enforced, and unknown catalogue entities now return 404.

No customer-level data, credentials, tokens, or production secrets are included in this report.

## 4. Findings not confirmed or corrected during audit

- `robots.txt` was not missing. It already existed and was valid; it needed only an `/admin` rule and a separate deny-all admin robots policy.
- Sitemap splitting was not required. Even the 5,252-URL baseline was below the 50,000 URL limit; quality filtering was the urgent issue.
- “Out of stock” was not treated as an automatic reason to noindex. The evaluator permits legitimate on-order pages when content, identity, price, image, uniqueness, and claims pass.
- No credible Google positions, keyword volumes, or difficulty values were available. The keyword map states that ranking is not reliably measurable.
- No safe basis existed to invent pharmacists, experts, certifications, citations, locations, or social profiles. Unsupported statements were removed or quarantined instead.

## 9. Indexation policy

A product can be indexed only when it is published and has a canonical-safe identity, valid brand/category, sufficiently useful description, controlled non-placeholder image, valid positive price, internally consistent availability, unique normalized title, and no blocked generic/absolute claims. Stock zero alone is not a blocker. Failed products remain live for users and are `noindex,follow`.

Brands and categories enter the sitemap only when indexable and backed by at least one eligible published product. Query/facet/pagination variants are `noindex,follow` and canonicalize to their clean landing route. Search, cart, checkout, account, favorites, API, and admin areas are excluded as appropriate. Articles require publication/indexability; the current 20 require genuine expert review before re-entry.

## 10. Structured data

- `OnlineStore`/Organization: real site identity and COD/cash payment only.
- `Product`: real name, images, brand, offer price/currency/availability, URL, and identifiers when present.
- `BreadcrumbList`: product and applicable content templates, aligned to canonical slugs.
- `Article`: retained on article detail pages, while unreviewed pages are noindexed.
- `AggregateRating` and `Review`: emitted only from reviews passing order eligibility; currently absent because no production review qualifies.

All JSON-LD blocks on the production homepage and two representative PDPs parsed successfully. Rich Results web UI testing remains a manual follow-up because the in-app browser was unavailable.
