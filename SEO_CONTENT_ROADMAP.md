## 1. Funnel Architecture Overview
The core objective is to guide users seamlessly through the intent funnel:
```text
INFORMATIONAL SEARCH QUERY (Google)
          ↓
IN-DEPTH EDUCATIONAL / COMPARISON ARTICLE (/conseils/[slug])
          ↓ (Natural contextual links + Category CTA badges)
COMMERCIAL PILLAR CATEGORY (/whey-proteine, /creatine, /pre-workout, etc.)
          ↓ (Rich product cards, live stock, dynamic prices)
AUTHENTIC PRODUCT PAGE (/produits/[slug])
          ↓
ORGANIC CONVERSION / ORDER (24-48h Delivery across Tunisia)
```
