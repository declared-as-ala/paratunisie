Public observations:

- [Protein.tn whey collection](https://protein.tn/whey-proteine) uses the whey/Tunisie category vocabulary and comparison framing. [Its creatine collection](https://protein.tn/creatine) is separated from [its price guide](https://protein.tn/blog/prix-de-la-creatine-en-tunisie).
- [Vitamin Shop's whey category](https://www.vitaminshop.tn/product-category/whey/), [Gainlab's whey collection](https://gainlabnutrition.com/collections/whey-tunisie), and [Muscle Rock's whey category](https://musclerocknutrition.tn/categories/whey-proteins) reinforce category + product-format purchase intent. Their wording was not copied.
- [Parapharmacie Plus vitamins](https://parapharmacieplus.tn/c/211/vitamines) and [Eden Pharma supplements](https://edenpharma.tn/fr/categorie/complement-alimentaire) use distinct supplement families, visible prices and product listings. This supports retaining specific category pages rather than one undifferentiated keyword landing page.

Content deliberately avoids “best”, “original”, “official”, “certified”, numerical medical outcomes or delivery promises unless supported by the particular product or business information. Category advice focuses on comparable units, packaging, declared ingredients, allergen labels and equipment compatibility. Existing product descriptions are preserved structurally and cleaned of obsolete shop advertising; they are not treated as independently verified clinical evidence.

[ANSES's assessment of supplements for athletes](https://www.anses.fr/fr/system/files/NUT2014SA0008Ra.pdf) supports caution about blanket supplement recommendations. New copy does not prescribe a universal dose or claim guaranteed performance, weight-loss or hormonal effects. This source is not used to certify individual catalogue products.

Technical references:

- [Google ecommerce pagination](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/specialty/ecommerce/pagination-and-incremental-page-loading): crawlable page links and distinct pagination canonicals.
- [Google merchant listings](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/merchant-listing): real product, price, currency, availability and image data.
- [Google structured-data policies](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/sd-policies): markup must reflect the page and its actual content.
- [Google FAQ guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2023/08/howto-faq-changes): FAQ markup is not a promise of a visible FAQ search feature. FAQs are included for shoppers where useful.

The generated keyword lists are editorial intent mappings, not measured search demand. The first term is the primary keyword; subsequent terms are secondary or related expressions. The admin exposes that distinction. Its completeness score is an internal checklist, not a Google ranking factor.
